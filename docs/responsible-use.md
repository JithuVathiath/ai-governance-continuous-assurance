# Responsible Use

Aegis is suitable for education, portfolio demonstration, policy-engine prototyping, and controlled governance workflow experiments. It must not be presented as a certification product or used as the sole basis for deploying, procuring, rejecting, approving, or continuing an AI system.

Before organisational use:

- obtain qualified legal and regulatory interpretation for the actual jurisdiction, role, system, and date;
- involve affected stakeholders and the relevant worker, consumer, accessibility, equality, security, privacy, model-risk, and domain specialists;
- replace synthetic examples and self-declared approval states with authoritative evidence sources and accountable sign-off;
- validate policy packs and severities under controlled change management;
- secure evidence, identity, secrets, audit data, backups, retention, and incident handling;
- independently test whether controls work in practice, not merely whether a document exists;
- maintain a clear appeal, override, suspension, and human-review process.

The included scenarios are fictional. Their organisations, suppliers, evidence, findings, and dates are illustrative and contain no personal or confidential data.
